04 — How I lead

The win-win isn't soft.
It's the rational outcome.

When two sides each get what they actually need, the deal holds and the team keeps building. Getting there is a discipline — communication and reasoning, applied on purpose.

01

Make the reasoning visible

Decisions hold when everyone can see the logic. I write it down, name the trade-offs, and let the argument — not the org chart — win.

02

Influence without authority

I rarely own the teams I need. So I lead with clarity and credibility: understand each stakeholder's real interest, then find the path that serves all of them.

03

Design for leverage

One shared framework beats ten one-offs. I build reusable systems — a player SDK, a BRD template, a metadata model — so the next win comes cheaper.

04

Clear the path, then get out of it

My job is to unblock the people doing the work and hand them the credit. Servant leadership isn't a slogan — it's how good people stay.

05

Steelman the other side

Before I argue against a position, I try to state it better than the person holding it. You can't find the win-win until you actually understand the other side's win.
